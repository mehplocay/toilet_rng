## Findings fixed

| ID / severity | Description | Failure scenario before fix | Minimal fix | Regression evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A4-01 / High | Transactions can exploit the wallet's large representable spacing. The former debit fallback only handled a completely unchanged wallet. | At 2^53 coins, 100 three-coin collection/sale fixture awards increased the wallet by 400 while consuming only 300 nominal coins. Other price/spacing ratios rounded purchases downward without triggering the old fallback. | `CashMath` recovers the binary64 sum residual. Debits/source subtraction round conservatively; credits correct material upward rounding. All wallet award paths use the shared credit helper; capacity checks use conservative room. | Repeated collection/sale cases; representable-step debit/credit tests across exponents 54..994; retained huge purchase/receipt/admin checks. |
| A4-02 / Medium | Pending settlement frequency changes income through upward rounding. | Basic Poop adds 6000 subcoins/second. At 2^65 pending units, short settlements rounded each addition up to 8192; repeated short ticks gained more than the configured rate. | Pending additions use the same bounded credit arithmetic. Existing timestamp consumption, offline windows and 6000-unit scale stay intact. | 100 one-second settlements cannot exceed 100 configured rate increments; existing online/offline and normal balance simulations retained. |
| A4-03 / Medium | Rejected product requests bypassed response pacing. | 2000 malformed/rate-rejected `BuyProduct` calls produced 2000 result packets although no purchase was admitted. | A per-player two-second rejection-response cooldown covers surplus arguments and failed admission. Normal admitted purchase feedback remains; departure removes cooldown state. | 2000 mixed invalid requests produce at most three responses, no prompt and no DataStore write; purchase feedback UI tests retained. |
| A4-04 / Medium | Per-player busy flags did not bound outstanding platform work across departed sessions. | Hold a group, friendship, policy or ownership backend request; repeatedly join/leave. Group/policy/ownership tests retained 40 suspended workers after their player maps were removed. | Shared group/friend capacity 12; separate policy and ownership capacities 12 each. A slot is released only when its protected API call returns, even if the player already left. No waiting queue; late results cannot restore departed state. | Four backend variants, 40 departures each, capacity/cleanup checks, counter release and social recovery; existing late-completion/race tests retained. |

The arithmetic contract remains deliberately approximate above 2^53. A credit's permitted positive rounding error is at most `amount * 2^-48`, never `wallet * epsilon`; the 33%/36% rounding gains above are eliminated. This preserves the existing tight large-grant tests without widening `CashMath.Near` or relaxing assertions. Corrections can lose less than one representable wallet step; very small accumulation can round away. A paid credit that cannot move the wallet still defers intact. Affordability has no epsilon, purchases charge at least the price, and no new progression cap, price, multiplier or schema was introduced. Lifetime `Earned` remains an approximate statistic.

## Residual risks and owner checklist

No live Studio or published billing/DataStore test was performed. Platform caches may retain stale group/friend relationships despite a refresh. Referral metadata is platform attribution, not proof of a unique human or an unforgeable reward credential. The qualification/daily/lifetime bounds reduce farming; legitimate alts still qualify. No new account-age rule was invented. Cached verified pass ownership remains monotonic and does not reconcile refunds. A policy change can remain unknown until refresh/expiry (120-second validity); backend saturation never extends that deadline.

Above 2^53, the design cannot promise exact coins, subcoin tails or exact displayed transaction deltas. Tiny credits may disappear statistically or defer; a tiny price can cost a representable wallet step. The pending ledger's 1e300-unit bound corresponds to approximately 1.6667e296 coins at the unchanged scale. This review does not certify Roblox's serialization of every huge finite double. Ordinary unsaved free progress can be lost in a crash. Existing lease/archive protection depends on preserving durable records; external rollback or deleting tombstones can break receipt recovery.

Activity reports can be automated and network-owned movement can be manipulated. Neither AFK pacing nor plot distance is a general bot/movement detector. Suspended platform calls cannot be forcibly assumed canceled: at most 36 lookup slots across the three budgets can remain occupied indefinitely during a backend outage; new lookups wait for a later retry rather than building a queue. Load/receipt DataStore behavior still needs native fault/queue testing. Headless tests do not prove GPU, physics, bandwidth or heap performance.

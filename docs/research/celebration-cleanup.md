# Celebration cleanup (2026-10-07)

- `Instance:Destroy()` disposes a UI effect and its children; external RunService/player/remote connections still need explicit `Disconnect()`. Attribute-specific signals allow prompt entitlement/plot invalidation without scanning Workspace. Sources: https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/classes/Instance and https://create.roblox.com/docs/scripting/events/deferred . Deferred callbacks require idempotent teardown; the HUD lives outside the destroyed Frame.
- Character removal is distinct from death. Observe CharacterAdded/CharacterRemoving and Humanoid.Died, including a Humanoid inserted after CharacterAdded. A bounded heartbeat also guards missing/replaced characters and zero health. Sources: https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/classes/Player#CharacterRemoving and https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/classes/Humanoid#Died .
- Disabling an emitter is not immediate removal of emitted particles; Clear removes them. Trail has a separate Clear method. The current Dance Pack owns UI Frames only, so destroying its own Frames is sufficient; it starts no ParticleEmitter, Trail, Sound, AnimationTrack or camera effect. Sources: https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/classes/ParticleEmitter and https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/classes/Trail . DevForum example of residual-particle cleanup: https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-camera-tool-emit-a-flash/1925844 .
- Checked release notes 700/701: deferred Close signals and a HapticEffect destruction fix; neither establishes a different lifecycle for these UI Frames. Sources: https://create.roblox.com/docs/release-notes/release-notes-700 and https://create.roblox.com/docs/release-notes/release-notes-701 .
- Luau if-expressions preserve false values, useful for independent ON/OFF states: https://rfcs.luau.org/syntax-if-expression.html .
- Tool references: Rojo binary-place builds use `.rbxl`: https://rojo.space/docs/v0.5/getting-started/new-game/ ; v7 project format: https://rojo.space/docs/v7/project-format/ . StyLua Windows line endings use CRLF: https://github.com/JohnnyMorganz/StyLua ; historic override issue: https://github.com/JohnnyMorganz/StyLua/issues/925 (installed 2.5.2). Selene Roblox std support depends on its build/features: https://kampfkarren.github.io/selene/roblox.html and https://kampfkarren.github.io/selene/cli/installation.html .

Repository findings: CatalogEffects' HUD button only opened/closed the menu, never stopped a selection or changed its label. Menu stop/selection-off canceled future bursts but left existing Frames until their 1.4-second expiry. Dance Pack and Confetti Reveal shared one pieces/style/age buffer; every Flush result could replace it and look like celebration continuing. Repeated Auto-Flush can keep generating that separate paid reveal. Death, plot change, rebirth and ownership revocation did not immediately clear the Dance Pack burst. CosmeticService applies DancePack as an ownership attribute only; no Celebrating attribute or celebration remote exists. Rainbow Name, paid/rebirth trails, companions and world-event effects are independent entitlements, not Dance Pack resources.

Fix policy: Celebrate starts Sparkles and shows the menu; Stop removes all four Dance Pack channels and any currently visible Confetti Reveal burst synchronously. Independent switches stay visible as ON/OFF until the menu is closed. Press Stop then Celebrate to reopen it. Explicit user selection is required after lifecycle resets. A later successful flush can still trigger the independent Confetti Reveal entitlement, whose separate one-shot channel expires within 1.4 seconds. Both channels honor RevealIntensity, with a combined maximum of 48 Frames in Full and 16 in Reduced, and updates at 10 Hz. No monetization/remotes/rate-limit changes.

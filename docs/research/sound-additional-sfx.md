- [Current Creator Hub audio requirements](https://create.roblox.com/docs/audio/assets) accept OGG, WAV, MP3 or FLAC, one stream, under 20 MB/seven minutes and at most 48 kHz. They do not require WAV. Deliver the new batch as **48 kHz mono Vorbis**, keeping the existing 21 files at their original 32 kHz. Vorbis has no 16-bit PCM storage-depth field; the brief's 16-bit WAV clause is superseded by the task's explicit OGG delivery instruction. 9 MB per batch is the project handoff budget.

Full-page access hit a JavaScript challenge; no file-format or stop-envelope guarantee is inferred from that snippet. The current Creator Hub requirements above remain the import authority.
- [Sound.Stop](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/classes/Sound#Stop) does not promise a user-controllable sample-level release. Local read-only inspection shows `AudioService:Stop` → `_forget` → `_release` destroys the voice. **Engineering conclusion:** any nonzero waveform can have a discontinuity when cut at an arbitrary phase; file authoring cannot eliminate that at every possible stop point. Handoff: apply approximately 20 ms gain release before destroying RebirthHold/Ambience, and a short attack on fresh starts. No runtime edit is authorized here. Testing 64 phases with an offline cosine release verifies the asset is release-ready, not that the existing runtime performs that release.
- [Xiph Vorbis specification](https://www.xiph.org/vorbis/doc/Vorbis_I_spec.html) describes overlap reconstruction and sample-granular stream boundaries. The new generator checks decoded amplitude, first-difference continuity and local RMS at the wrap, beyond exact decoded frame counts. Codec reconstruction at file edges differs slightly from the periodic source; deterministically trying circular cuts within 24 ms finds a cleaner encoded seam without trimming samples or baking a fade-to-silence notch into the loop.
- [SciPy Fourier resampling documentation](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.signal.resample.html) describes Fourier interpolation's periodicity assumption and even-length Nyquist-bin handling. No SciPy installation is used: the existing 32 kHz palette is composed into new, band-limited signals and interpolated to 48 kHz with NumPy FFTs. One-shots have smooth zero boundaries; sustain loops are circular. The old Nyquist bin is split during upsampling. An analytic 1 kHz fixture checks time, amplitude and rate conversion; final headroom/edge checks follow Vorbis decoding.
- [Rojo v7 build instructions](https://rojo.space/docs/v7/getting-started/new-game/) document `rojo build -o build.rbxl` for a binary place build. No project/source modifications are required for this verification.

## Iteration findings

- The first render exposed decoded endpoint overshoot in Sell, DisplayPlace and AutoFlushToggle. Increasing their smooth onset to 7 ms passed the tighter 0.001 FS edge check without adding leading silence. Sell/SellAll also pass offline 1.00–1.24x playback-speed probes.
- Initial RebirthSuccess orchestration had excessive crest factor. Longer overlapping fanfare notes plus quieter, staggered closing keys and sustained pads brought estimated loudness close to -15 LUFS while retaining more than 3 dB true-peak headroom. No limiter was added.
- Initial loop cuts passed the original 0.003 FS limit but failed the new 0.001 amplitude/slope limits. Codec-aware circular cuts fix this without loop gaps. The manifest records the final cut and stop limitations explicitly.
- Contact-sheet inspection found the initial distant birds too buried visually in the ocean bed. Raised only those three gentle gestures; the ambient bed retains its quiet target and no musical cadence. This remains a synthetic approximation, pending actual listening.

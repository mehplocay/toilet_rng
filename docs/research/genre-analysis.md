Research date: 2026-10-05. Design inputs, not a claim that copying a hit guarantees retention. Historical examples are dated; product prices and private analytics are not inferred. Recommendations below are our hypotheses.

## Session lengths, cadence and measurement

**No verified per-game average/median session lengths, D1/D7 or ARPPU were found in the reviewed public sources.** Do not substitute CCU, time-to-hatch or update attendance for these metrics.

| Layer | Evidence | Proposed Toilet RNG target, not a genre fact |
|---|---|---|
| First action | [Creator Hub engagement](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/engagement) emphasizes getting to the fun quickly and inspecting minute-by-minute exits | First flush within 15 seconds; first meaningful choice within 90 seconds |
| Normal visit | Same source defines average session time; averages can hide idle users | 8-15 active minutes; optional 20-30 minute co-op visit; 60-minute script is coverage, not required attendance |
| Return visit | Garden interview supports offline progress and weekly appointments | A useful 3-5 minute check-in; daily checklist takes 8-12 minutes; no streak loss |
| Live cadence | Garden interview: weekly Saturday updates; PS99 historical seasonal event: every 4 hours | One small content release per week, major content every 4 weeks; repeat event opportunities across time zones |
| Benchmarking | [Creator Hub analytics dashboard](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/analytics-dashboard) uses changing similar-player comparison groups | Compare current dashboard cohorts; do not cite its illustrative percentages as universal targets |
| Evaluation | [Roblox experiments](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/experiments) defines session, retention, conversion and ARPPU metrics | Track first-flush completion, 5/10-minute survival, first upgrade, return cohorts, payer conversion and purchase regret/support tickets |

Initial internal gates: >=85% of loaded new players flush within 30 seconds; >=65% buy Dirty within 5 active minutes; D1 >=20%, D7 >=8%, D30 >=3%. These are **planning thresholds**, not forecasts or verified competitor results. Review at >=1,000 new users per cohort, wait for full retention windows, report confidence intervals and mobile/desktop splits. Never optimize session length by forced waiting or automatic rejoining.

## Policy findings and design implications

Primary sources checked: [paid random items policy](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/paid-random-items), [PolicyService API](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/classes/PolicyService), [monetization guidance](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization).

- Paid randomness includes indirect purchases and probability modifiers. Lucky Flush and paid server luck therefore need eligibility gating and disclosure.
- Show every outcome and its actual percentage before purchase; provide a labeled details control. Disclose modifier effects and update enhanced odds. Underlying probabilities total 100%; follow the policy's rounding guidance.
- Check `ArePaidRandomItemsRestricted` per user; check `IsPaidItemTradingAllowed` for paid items and resulting outcomes. Free acquisition does not authorize a restricted paid interaction.
- Avoid false scarcity, resetting sale countdowns and pressure language aimed at minors.

Our stricter product decisions: no paid currency feeding RNG, no permanent paid luck/speed, no paid-item trading at initial trading launch, fail closed for monetized randomness on policy lookup failure, and no purchased server modifier for restricted recipients. Give those recipients a deterministic cosmetic celebration. These are implementation choices, not quoted platform requirements.

The proposed odds UI must use the existing rarest-first algorithm: `P(item i) = min(1,L/X_i) * product(1-min(1,L/X_j))` for all earlier checks j; Poop gets the remaining probability. A displayed base `1/X` alone is not the player's actual chance.

## What this research does not establish

- No causal proof that daily rewards, paid luck or weekly updates alone improve retention.
- No verified current competitor price list, revenue estimate or recommended spending level.
- No reliance on historical DevForum advice to farm idle payouts or on old discovery tests as current algorithm guarantees.
- Recheck the official policy and relevant API/release notes when implementing purchases, trading or capture APIs. This task changes documentation only.
